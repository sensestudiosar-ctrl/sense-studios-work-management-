Production accounting-provider binding is pending.

Current internal identity/session, organization/project/task assignment, explicit cross-branch access and scoped grants are backend enforced. CLIENT access is excluded. Optional evidence uses DOC authorization; inaccessible historical references are redacted and added/replaced correction evidence is reauthorized. NAS paths/credentials/evidence bytes do not enter accounting payloads.
