# Approved clarifications and addenda

The original 115-section requirements remain unchanged at APPROVED_REQUIREMENTS.md. This file records newer owner instructions without erasing source history. In conflicts, the newer explicit clarification takes precedence.

## C-001 — Branch-only organizational model (approved 2026-09-25)

The organizational hierarchy is **Organization → Branch → Team → Employee**. One organization may have multiple branches. Each project has exactly one owning branch. Employees belong to the appropriate organizational/branch structure; explicitly authorized project assignments may permit cross-branch work.

There is **no separate Brand model, entity, table, permission scope or architectural layer**. Any source use of “Brand” or “multi-brand” describing organizational partitioning and isolation is superseded by Branch and branch isolation. This applies to introductory text and source sections R-004, R-006, R-007, R-092, R-093, R-094, R-103 and R-113. Other uses of role, team, project membership and explicit permission overrides remain meaningful and are not renamed.

The original source lists “Departments” and “Hierarchy” as organizational layers. The approved hierarchy has no Department level. Reporting-manager relationships and dynamic job roles may still be needed for Team Leader authority and assignment recommendations, but they must live within the approved Organization/Branch/Team/Employee structure, not create another organizational tier. Their exact home-membership cardinality and cross-branch grant authority were resolved by C-002 Q1 and Q15 below.

This clarification changes organizational structure only. It does not remove any project, task, review, file, workforce, site, portal, analytics, automation or AI capability from the original source.

## C-003 — Fixed retention schedule for Q8 (approved 2026-09-25)

The owner selected follow-up Schedule A for Q8. Retain client approval evidence, issued drawings and audit records for **10 years**; exited-employee records for **7 years after exit**; and site attendance selfies for **90 days**. The attendance event may remain after its selfie expires, subject to the applicable employee-record policy. This completes the duration choice in C-002 Q8 and the selfie duration needed for Q11.

The schedule's legal and contract fit must be checked before production. Record expiry clock start, legal-hold exceptions, backup expiry and controlled purge/audit behavior in the final architecture. For planning, use project closure for approvals/drawings, event time for audit/selfies and exit date for employee records; these clock anchors are implementation proposals, not additional owner-approved durations. No application behavior or deletion job is implemented. C-009 subsequently approved the clock anchors and controlled expiry policy.

## C-004 — Leave-year and entitlement policy (approved 2026-09-25)

Work from Home remains a schedule/attendance change and does not consume leave. The leave year runs January 1–December 31. The full-year paid-leave allowance is set by the Owner per Branch and credited in full at the beginning of the leave year. A new hire's first-year allowance is prorated by calendar day for the remaining year. If an employee changes home Branch during the year, entitlement is calculated by calendar days under each Branch's effective policy. Up to five unused days carry forward into the next leave year and expire at the end of that next year. The actual full-year allowance for each Branch must be entered and approved before leave balances are used; no number is invented here. Historical policy versions and calculations must remain explainable.

## C-005 — Score policy detail (approved 2026-09-25)

Employee performance uses separate Owner-approved formulas for a small number of role groups, such as design, site and leadership. Project health uses separate Owner-approved formulas for Design and Turnkey projects. The Owner must enter and approve the initial weights before any score appears. Formula weights, thresholds, exclusions and effective dates are versioned under C-002 Q12; prior published scores are not silently recalculated. Exact numerical policies remain setup data to be approved by the Owner before the respective scoring feature is enabled.

## C-006 — External CAD sharing (approved 2026-09-25)

Clients receive PDF/image files only. Explicitly assigned vendors and consultants may receive CAD source files through a native MinisCloud share link for a specific file. Only the Owner may issue the link. Every link has a seven-day expiry and an extraction code, and its recipient, file/revision, issue time, expiry and issuer are recorded in the app. When recipient project access ends, the Owner cancels any active link in MinisCloud immediately and records the cancellation in the app. The approved flow uses native NAS capabilities; no unverified automatic MinisCloud API control or unrestricted NAS folder access is assumed. A native link may be forwarded, so the extraction code and expiry limit exposure but do not bind download to one identity.

## C-007 — Backup and recovery target (approved 2026-09-25)

Keep nightly encrypted backups of the application database and files on an always-reachable computer or NAS at a different physical location. The Owner accepts up to one day of recent work loss after total primary-NAS loss. NAS snapshots remain useful for local rollback but are not the offsite backup. The specific offsite host, connection, key custody, monitoring and restore drill are deployment details to verify before production; no backup mechanism has been implemented.

## C-008 — Sign-in, approval step-up and launch messaging (approved 2026-09-25)

Invited clients and consultants sign in to their restricted portals with an emailed one-time code. Formal client approval additionally requires the approving client to use a registered passkey; viewing permitted PDF/image files does not. The Owner and employees use passkeys. Lost employee passkeys require Owner verification and authorization before replacement, with lost credentials revoked. The Owner registers a second passkey and keeps an offline recovery code away from the NAS. The app may use Brevo's free transactional email plan for sign-in codes and essential email; the Owner accepts its free-plan footer at launch. Launch notifications use in-app alerts and email, without automated SMS/WhatsApp. The free-plan send limit, delivery, domain authentication and fallback on mail outage must be validated before portal launch. Email codes are lower-assurance than passkeys and do not authorize formal client approval by themselves.

## C-009 — Retention clock and controlled expiry (approved 2026-09-25)

The C-003 ten-year clock for client approval evidence and issued drawings starts when the associated project closes. The ten-year clock for each audit event starts when that event occurs. Exited-employee record retention starts at exit, and the ninety-day site-selfie clock starts at capture. At expiry, deletion is scheduled with advance Owner notice; a documented hold may prevent deletion while a contract or dispute requires it. The final design must account for holds, linked records, backup expiry and proof of deletion. This is a product policy, not a conclusion that the periods comply with every applicable law or contract; legal and contract review remains a production gate.

## C-010 — Project cancellation authority (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-01 option A. Only the Owner may make a Draft, Active or On Hold project Cancelled. A Project Lead or Team Leader may request cancellation but cannot make it effective. Cancellation requires a reason and immutable actor/time/audit evidence. Cancelled is terminal under source R-016; prior project, task, review, approval and file history is preserved. This decision does not authorize cancelling a Completed or Archived project. Handling of unfinished tasks and remaining external access after cancellation are separate dependencies. C-011 resolves the task behavior; C-012 resolves vendor/consultant access; C-013 fixes the client's read-only portal scope; C-014 requires automatic expiry with Owner-approved extensions, and C-015 clarifies that the Owner sets an end date separately for each client's project access. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-011 — Unfinished delivery tasks on project cancellation (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-02 option A. When the Owner cancels a project, every unfinished delivery task on that project becomes **Stopped due to project cancellation** in the same logical cancellation operation. Completed tasks remain Completed. The stop is terminal for delivery work: it removes those tasks from active work, overdue reminders and active capacity while retaining assignments, time entries, submissions, reviews, revisions, comments and audit history. The cancellation reason, actor and time link the project and affected task transitions. If legitimate wrap-up work remains, an authorized Project Lead or Team Leader under C-016 creates a separately identified closeout task linked to the Cancelled project; this does not reopen the project or any stopped delivery task. The closeout task follows normal task ownership, access, time and audit rules and must be distinguished from delivery work in workload and reporting. This extends source R-024 task states without changing the verbatim source. C-012 resolves vendor/consultant access after cancellation; C-013 fixes the client's read-only portal scope; C-014 requires automatic expiry with Owner-approved extensions, and C-015 clarifies that the Owner sets an end date separately for each client's project access. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-012 — Vendor and consultant access at project cancellation (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-03 option A. When the Owner's cancellation of a project takes effect, assigned vendors and consultants lose app/portal access to that project's work and files. Any active native MinisCloud CAD links for that project must be cancelled by the Owner as part of the cancellation process, with the link identity, revocation confirmation, actor and time recorded. Since C-006 does not establish an automatic MinisCloud revocation API, the application must not treat an unconfirmed native link as revoked. The project cancellation stays pending until active native links are confirmed revoked; the final project/task/access transition is then recorded with C-010/C-011 evidence. An authorized Owner may revoke an app grant earlier while cancellation is pending. Later return, cleanup or handover work by a vendor/consultant requires a separately approved, narrow closeout access arrangement under C-016, not continued access to the cancelled delivery project. C-013 fixes the client's read-only portal scope after cancellation; C-014 requires automatic expiry, with C-015 later clarifying per-client, per-project end dates. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-013 — Client portal scope after project cancellation (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-04 option A. After an Owner cancellation takes effect, the project's existing authorized Primary and Additional Client users retain read-only portal access only to PDF/image revisions previously formally issued to their client scope and to their own approval history. Existing item-specific grants and exact-revision checks still apply; one client's files or approval evidence never become visible to another client. The portal must not expose working drafts, internal records, CAD files, new transmittals, or new project decisions. Pending client review/approval requests become Withdrawn due to project cancellation with their prior events and evidence retained; new reviews, comments, approvals, invitations and uploads are disabled. A previously issued revision remains visibly marked Superseded—Do Not Use where applicable. The Owner's existing permission-revocation authority remains available. C-014 fixes automatic expiry and Owner-approved extension for this limited read-only access; C-015 later clarifies that there is no shared period; the Owner sets each client's end date. Approved record-retention rules do not by themselves grant portal access. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-014 — Automatic expiry of cancelled-project client access (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-05 option B. The limited read-only client portal access approved by C-013 ends automatically after a finite post-cancellation period for each client grant, as clarified by C-015. The clock begins when the Owner's project cancellation takes effect, not when a cancellation request is made. After expiry, the client cannot view or download issued files or approval history through that project portal unless the Owner approves a documented extension; any extension has a new explicit end time and is audited. The Owner may revoke access earlier. Every protected request must enforce the current end time, so a delayed scheduled job cannot leave expired access usable. Portal expiry does not delete the underlying issued drawings, approval evidence or audit records; C-003/C-009 retention and legal holds remain separate. At C-014 the exact period remained OD-06; C-015 later resolves this with an Owner-set end date for each client's project access. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-015 — Owner-set end date for each cancelled-project client grant (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner clarified OD-06: there is no organization-wide 90-day, 12-month, 3-year or other shared period for the C-013 read-only portal access. At cancellation, the Owner sets a separate end date for each currently authorized Primary or Additional Client portal account's grant to that project. Each end date must be later than the effective cancellation time and is required before cancellation can be finalized. Different clients and invited client users may have different dates; their existing project, item and document scopes do not expand. The Owner may shorten, revoke or extend a grant later, with reason, actor, previous/new end time and audit evidence retained. Access ends automatically at the grant's current end time, enforced on every request. This clarifies C-014's automatic expiry and Owner extension without altering the independent record-retention policy. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-016 — Closeout work approval after project cancellation (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-07 option B. Once a project is Cancelled, its assigned Project Lead or a Team Leader with an explicit project closeout-approval grant may authorize an ordinary, separately identified closeout task linked to that project. Approval records the reason, work scope, task owner, due date, approver and time; the task and its time/review history remain distinct from stopped delivery work. The same authority may approve a separate vendor/consultant portal grant only for the named closeout task, work package and permitted files, with a required end time; the grant ends on expiry, closeout completion or earlier revocation, and does not restore the vendor's original project access. A Team Leader's title alone does not grant this authority. The Owner may revoke a closeout approval or grant. C-010 Owner-only project cancellation, C-002 Owner-only cross-branch employee grants, C-006 Owner-only native CAD link issuance/cancellation and all other protected approval rules remain in force. Closeout work cannot reopen the Cancelled project, restart stopped delivery tasks, issue new client approvals or silently expand client portal access. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-017 — Forward lead-stage skips (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-08 option A. A person with the lead-stage-change permission and access to the specific lead may move it from its current nonterminal sales stage directly to a later stage when intermediate steps did not occur. A reason is required. The stage-change record retains the previous and new stage, each skipped intermediate stage, actor and time; skipped stages are marked as skipped, never as completed meetings, proposals or other activities. Sales reports distinguish skipped steps from completed steps and retain the original stage history. Existing R-010 requirements still apply: Lost needs a reason, and Won may convert to a Project. This clarification does not authorize general reverse-stage moves or settle whether a Lost lead can reopen; OD-09 addresses the returning-Lost-lead choice. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-018 — Returning Lost enquiry creates a linked new lead (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-09 option B. A lead marked Lost remains Lost, with its loss reason, outcome date, interactions, stage history and audit evidence retained. When the same person returns with a renewed enquiry, an authorized staff member creates a separate lead linked to the earlier Lost lead. The new lead receives its own identity, stage history, outcome and reporting period; the old lead is not reopened or reclassified. Staff may review relevant earlier contact and proposal context only within their existing access grants; linking two leads does not expand cross-branch visibility or copy old approvals into the new opportunity. C-017 still permits a reasoned forward jump if the renewed enquiry starts at a later stage. This clarification does not decide whether an active lead may move backward after a real change; OD-10 addresses that. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-019 — Reasoned backward stage moves for active leads (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-10 option A. A person with the lead-stage-change permission and access to the specific lead may move it from its current nonterminal sales stage to an earlier nonterminal stage when real sales work returns there, such as Negotiation back to Proposal after a scope change. A reason, actor and time are required. The previous stage entries and all activities remain intact; the current stage changes, while reports and audit history show both the earlier progress and the backward move rather than rewriting or deleting it. This is distinct from correcting a mistaken entry, which also requires an audited correction. The move does not revoke an issued proposal, undo a client decision, or reopen a Won/Lost lead. C-017 forward skips and C-018 new linked leads for returning Lost enquiries remain in force. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-020 — Named independent verifier for each site snag (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-11 option A. The assigned Project Lead or a Team Leader with explicit authority for that project appoints a named verifier for each snag. The verifier must already have appropriate access to the project's snag and must be a different person from the person who performed the repair; appointment does not create cross-branch project access. Assignment and reassignment record the actor, time and reason. Only the current named verifier may record whether a repair passes or fails verification, with inspection note and available photo/file evidence retained. A failed check returns the same snag to In Progress for further repair and preserves every earlier attempt; a passed check moves it to Verified. No snag may become Closed without a passed verification. The separate authority to move Verified to Closed remains OD-12. The site responsible person's R-069 daily-report confirmation does not by itself confer snag verification authority. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-021 — Management closes verified site snags (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-12 option B. A snag may move from Verified to Closed only when its latest verification attempt passed and the assigned Project Lead or a Team Leader with explicit authority for that project reviews that evidence and makes a separate closure decision. Record the closer, time, current verification reference and any closure note without deleting the verifier's inspection, earlier failed attempts or repair history. Being the named verifier alone does not grant closure authority. If the assigned Project Lead or authorized Team Leader also served as the named verifier, the closure still requires its own explicit authorized action and audit record; the Owner did not require a different person for closure. A failed or superseded verification cannot support closure. C-020 independent verification remains required. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-022 — Management approval for cancellation of approved leave (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-13 option B. An employee may request cancellation of previously approved leave, but the cancellation must receive approval at the same management level required for the original leave approval, including when the leave has not yet begun. A pending request does not restore leave balance, scheduled capacity or assignments. On approval, the app records a separate cancellation decision linked to the original approval, with requester, approver, reason, time and the eligible unused leave being reversed. The original approval and entitlement ledger remain as history; only unused leave may be restored, and any already taken time stays recorded. Recalculate future capacity and affected tasks, alert the appropriate manager and task owners, and prompt review of prior handovers rather than silently reassigning work. A denial preserves the approved leave unchanged. Work from Home remains a schedule/attendance request under C-004 and does not consume leave. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-023 — Unfinished tasks wait for individual reassignment at employee exit (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-14 option A. When an employee exits, the application immediately revokes that person's sessions and grants. Each unfinished task for which they are Primary Owner enters Waiting with a needs-reassignment reason and a manager-visible alert. The departed employee remains the recorded current Primary Owner while it waits, maintaining the one-owner invariant; they have no further access, and the task cannot progress or be submitted until an authorized manager explicitly selects a new eligible Primary Owner. Preserve the previous task state, prior owner, deadlines, submissions, approvals, reviews, comments, time entries and audit history. The reassignment records old/new owner, actor, reason and time; the task may resume only to a valid state after applicable review and access checks. Completed and terminal tasks remain unchanged. This does not decide what happens to an Active project whose departing employee was its Project Lead; OD-15 addresses that separately. No application behavior is implemented.

## C-024 — Project Lead exit places Active project On Hold (approved 2026-09-25)

The Owner selected OD-15 option A. When an employee who is the current Project Lead of an Active project exits, their access is revoked immediately and each such project moves to On Hold with a Project Lead exit reason, actor/time audit and alerts to the Owner and affected team. The former lead and prior decisions remain in dated history; the On Hold project may temporarily have no current accountable lead, while the requirement for exactly one current lead continues to apply to every Active project. New delivery decisions, formal issue and pending client review/approval actions pause during this exit hold; pending requests and all evidence remain intact rather than being withdrawn or deleted. The Owner appoints an eligible replacement Project Lead with required project and Branch permissions before the project may resume Active through the normal audited gate checks. C-023 separately places the departing employee's unfinished tasks in Waiting for individual manager reassignment. Existing client access to previously issued records remains subject to its normal grant rules; this On Hold event is not project cancellation. No application behavior is implemented.
