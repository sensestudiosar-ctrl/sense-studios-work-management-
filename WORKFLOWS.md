# Workflows and state machines — S01–S11 complete; S12 in progress

## Implemented S11 document base

Owner/assigned granted staff selects a project and provisions template folders or creates a folder. Storage failure remains FAILED and retryable. Upload initiation binds actor/project/folder/name/type/size/policy; each bounded binary chunk has SHA-256 and exact retry semantics. Finalization checks the manifest and physical content, then atomically registers immutable metadata with audit/event/outbox and marks the upload FINALIZED. Expired staging becomes ABORTED through Owner reconciliation. Every read/stream rechecks current assignment/permissions and exact Confidential ancestor/file grants. Only Owner classifies Confidential content and grants/revokes item access. No revision, issue, approval or client viewer transition is implemented by S11.

Specified sequences below come from the approved source plus approved clarifications C-001–C-024. Arrows show default progression; C-017 and C-019 define explicit lead-stage exceptions. Unsupported transitions are denied until a slice specifies their guards and tests. Every protected transition runs server-side validation, authorization and audit in one business transaction.

## S06 implemented workflows

Granted actors create NEW leads in an authorized branch, record interactions, and change stages with optimistic versions. Skips, backward moves and Lost require reasons. Won/Lost remain terminal; returning enquiries create separate linked leads under creation and prior-view authority. Owner edits shared clients/contacts with reason and version, preserving contact identities. Source managers use organization-scoped authority to add or activate/deactivate sources with reason and expected timestamp. Each command persists audit/event/outbox evidence; no automatic customer message or project creation is performed.

## S07A implemented workflow

The Owner creates a Draft Design or Turnkey project directly or from one Won lead. Won conversion inherits the lead's Branch and Client and is limited to one project per lead; direct creation has no invented lead provenance. The initial baseline scopes and Draft status evidence are retained. S07A has no status transition endpoint, effective cancellation, scope-change decision or lead assignment. The lifecycle policy is unit-tested as a pure guard for later persisted transitions; it does not itself change a project.

## S07 completed project workflow

The Owner assigns contributors, appoints one eligible Project Lead and activates a Draft project. Active projects may be put On Hold with reason and resumed only after an eligible lead is in place. S08 checks required project-plan milestones before Active delivery can become Completed. Active-to-Completed requires Owner authority or a permission-defined Team Leader with current project access, branch-scoped project.manage, and explicit branch-scoped project.gate_override, even when every required milestone is complete. If required milestones are incomplete, that Team Leader must also invoke the override path with a reason; exact incomplete milestone IDs and actor/version are recorded in immutable and canonical audit evidence. S03 defines Team Leader authority through explicit grant and scope, never job title. Completed projects may then be archived. Status and lead history are append-only and each transition emits canonical audit/domain/outbox evidence. An Owner transfer to another active Branch records from/to history, revokes contributors whose home Branch differs from the destination and holds an Active project if its lead loses access; regrant and replacement are explicit Owner actions. A formal later scope addition starts as a request against an Active or eligible On Hold project, goes to the Owner-named independent approver through S05, and becomes a distinct scope row only after approval; the baseline remains. If the approver is unavailable, the Owner withdraws the pending request with a reason, names a replacement, and the requester can submit again. Project Lead or granted staff may request cancellation, but effective cancellation remains disabled until S09's atomic task-stop workflow exists. S07 provides the project hold/replacement step for a future employee exit transaction; S03 continues to refuse full exit until S09 joins its task waits.

## S08 templates and project-plan workflow

Staff with organization-scoped template.view can read active templates and versions; template.manage allows creating a template and immutable versions. Initial Design and Turnkey stage defaults are reference choices. Versions specify delivery type, initial scopes, ordered stages/milestones and folder/naming intent. Delivery type cannot change between versions. Project creation from a selected active version requires matching delivery type/scopes and commits S07 project/baseline history with applied-version snapshot, plan items, retry receipt and audit/outbox evidence. Identical retries return prior result; conflicting key reuse is rejected. Later template edits do not change existing plans.

Project viewers with current S07 access can read plans and completion history. On Active projects, assigned Project Lead or staff with project.plan_complete can complete a stage/milestone with a reason. Each item completes once; stale project versions are rejected. Required incomplete milestones block project completion. The Owner or explicitly authorized, permission-defined Team Leader may complete the project. A Team Leader must hold both project.manage and project.gate_override at branch scope plus current project access. project.gate_override is required for any staff completion; when milestones are missing it records the reason and exact milestone IDs in audit evidence. This does not establish formal client approval or bypass other gates. Folder definitions are not physical NAS folders; templates do not execute tasks, reviews, client decisions, procurement, customer notifications, reports or automation in S08.

## S09 implemented task and cancellation workflows

Task creation requires title, type, priority, target date and one active primary owner. Staff creating a self-task remain its owner; selecting another owner or collaborators is governed by `task.assign` and manager/project scope. An optional plan item must belong to the same project. Distinct collaborators may have explicit effort estimates. Edits to an open task may change title, description, target/start dates, estimate, priority, plan item, tags and checklist under optimistic version checks; S09 does not commit a controlled due date.

The ordinary task command permits NOT_STARTED → IN_PROGRESS and IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED. Each requires a reason and expected version. Completion with any required review fails closed; review-state transitions await S13/S14. An Owner employee-exit command puts each unfinished owned task into WAITING, retains the old owner, records prior status and reason, and revokes access within the exit transaction. Terminal tasks are unchanged. An authorized manager must explicitly choose an eligible replacement; reassignment appends owner/status evidence and resumes NOT_STARTED only when that was the prior state, otherwise IN_PROGRESS. A task awaiting reassignment cannot be edited or progressed.

Only Owner may finalize project cancellation from Draft, Active or On Hold. The transaction updates project status, creates ProjectStatusChange evidence, stops unfinished non-closeout tasks and links their status evidence to that change; completed tasks are preserved and stopped tasks cannot resume. S09 has no native CAD links or client/vendor grants; future modules must extend cancellation atomically before introducing those access records. Employee exit creates a mandatory in-app Owner alert. When exit holds a project, active contributors with current project access receive a mandatory in-app hold notice.

A separate closeout command creates a CLOSEOUT task only against a Cancelled project. Approval is granted by the assigned Project Lead or currently authorized staff with `project.closeout_approve`, and captures work scope, assigned owner and target date. The task may progress only while approval remains active. Owner revocation disables further work. This does not reactivate the project or grant external access.

## S10A ordinary Waiting/resume

An authorized primary owner or Owner explicitly moves IN_PROGRESS to WAITING with a required reason/delay cause and optional known responsible party. The saved active state and immutable history are retained. A separate reasoned resume restores IN_PROGRESS and clears only current waiting metadata. Ordinary reassignment preserves Waiting and its current cause. Employee exit replaces it with mandatory reassignment Waiting; project cancellation stops unfinished delivery and clears current waiting metadata while preserving history. Review-required completion remains closed pending S13/S14. Configured ordinary Waiting notices remain pending.

## S10C finish-to-start links

Task B's current primary owner or a scoped manager with access to both tasks can add/remove a reasoned same-project dependency while B remains open. The database serializes graph edits, prevents active duplicates/cycles and retains removal history. If A is unfinished when B starts, the UI shows a warning and a separate Start anyway decision; the service records the exact predecessor set and acknowledgement in status/audit evidence. A later link does not rewrite that decision. When A completes and linked B is in ordinary Waiting, the task service creates a per-B event and in-app notice for B's current owner. The worker rechecks recipient authority before delivery; B stays Waiting until its owner explicitly resumes it. Owner-enabled per-Team ordinary Waiting notices go only to currently authorized scoped managers and Owner; delivery rechecks task, Team and actor access.

## S10D committed-date extension

The current primary owner may submit one pending request for a later committed date, selecting an eligible scoped manager or Owner from the server-filtered list and providing a reason. The selected approver separately approves or rejects with a reason; approval atomically changes the commitment and links its immutable date history to the decision, while rejection leaves the date unchanged. The Owner may withdraw a pending request with a reason. Ordinary date editing does not bypass extension approval. Closeout, terminal/exit-wait and ineligible project work remain excluded.

## S10E configured Waiting notices and timezone

The Owner may enable or disable ordinary Waiting alerts per Team using a reason and expected settings version; they are off by default. The source task owner's current home Team controls routing. On an ordinary wait, an in-app notice is routed to active Owners and current authorized scoped managers. Delivery rechecks current opt-in, task owner/team, exact Waiting cycle and recipient access, suppressing stale notices after resume, reassignment, exit, cancellation or grant revocation. This path is separate from forced-exit notices and S10C dependency-completion notices. The Owner may also change the versioned Organization IANA timezone with a reason; its initial value is Asia/Kolkata and recurrence dates use that setting.

## S10F recurring task generation

The current primary owner, Owner or an existing scoped task manager configures a DAILY, WEEKLY or MONTHLY rule for a persisted RECURRING source task with a future first occurrence date and reason. Rules may be paused/resumed separately, each with a reason and expected task/rule versions; resume keeps the original frequency anchor and begins on the next scheduled date strictly after the current organization-local date. The worker evaluates only today's local date, skips missed dates and creates at most one occurrence per rule/date. Monthly anchors on the 29th, 30th or 31st use the last day of shorter months while retaining the anchor. An exited/inactive owner or non-Active linked project suspends generation. Generated tasks begin Not started and copy approved current source fields, but not commitments, dependencies or another recurrence rule. Generation never resumes, completes or bypasses review of existing work.

## S12 revision register and formal issue — COMPLETE for development/local staging

An authorized project user registers an existing accessible S11 file as R0 with a document number, title and reason. Later revisions append a new unclaimed project file with series R or CR, a reason and the drawing's expected version. Numbers are allocated by the service under drawing locking; concurrent stale writes must refresh and retry. Every revision is a new immutable file/checksum record and starts WORKING/non-current. The register shows retained history and serves a revision through the revision-specific S11-authorized stream; a superseded record is labeled “Superseded — Do Not Use.”

S13 review producers may consume canonical review evidence to move the exact revision through Submitted/Reviewed. Exact internal approval can then select the eligible current revision and supersede the older current approval atomically. S12 exposes no generic state or approval write. The Owner or an assigned permission-authorized Team Leader may select a drawing for client review once with a reason and expected version; the selection is one-way in S12. A CR revision or selected drawing needs its exact S14 client approval before issue.

Formal issue checks the Active project, scoped issue grant, current exact Approved/Issued revisions, required canonical S13/S14 approval facts, and each named recipient's project and file access. The transaction retains the transmittal, exact revision/checksum/gate snapshot, sender, purpose, reason, recipients and acknowledgement requirement, changes newly issued revisions to ISSUED, and associates the issued blob with retention. The production HTTP command remains blocked when required S13/S14 facts are absent. The named internal recipient alone may acknowledge after current access is rechecked. When a project first becomes Completed or Cancelled, the earliest such status-change time anchors the ten-year retention metadata; archive/closeout does not reset it. S12 has no purge job, external delivery, client portal or S13/S14 approval workflow. Whole-slice acceptance is pending.
