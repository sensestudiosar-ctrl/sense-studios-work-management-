# S14 client review implementation contract

Status: implemented and verified for development/local staging; S14_COMPLETION_REPORT.md records acceptance evidence. Sources: R-024, R-031–R-034, R-037, R-039, R-075–R-076, R-104, C-002 Q5/Q7 and C-013–C-015. The Owner has settled independent CR content after its own internal review, Owner-only Primary appointment/replacement, and correction assignment by Owner, assigned Project Lead or an explicitly authorized Team Leader.

## Persistence interface

All models below have UUID `id`, `organizationId` and `projectId`. Dates are UTC timestamptz(3). Identity/content evidence is immutable; mutable state and grants require next `version` and canonical audit/event/outbox evidence. Exact schema field names are the following.

- `ClientReviewPackage`: `title`, `createdById`, `reason`, `createdAt`, `version`, `status` (`ClientReviewPackageStatus`: OPEN, APPROVED, WITHDRAWN).
- `ClientReviewItem`: `packageId`, `taskId`, nullable `revisionId`, `snapshot`, `label`, `status` (`ClientReviewItemStatus`: PENDING, IN_REVIEW, APPROVED, REVISION_REQUIRED, REJECTED, WITHDRAWN), `version`, `createdAt`. Unique package/task. A published item's task, revision, snapshot and label never change.
- `ClientItemDecision`: unique `itemId`, `accountId`, `kind` (`ClientItemDecisionKind`: APPROVED, REVISION_REQUIRED, REJECTED), nullable `signatureName`, nullable `consent`, `comment`, nullable `credentialId`, nullable `ipAddress`, nullable `userAgent`, `createdAt`, unique `auditEventId`. APPROVED requires nonempty signature name, consent=true and the approving account's active passkey credential.
- `ClientReviewComment`: `itemId`, `authorId`, `text`, `annotations` (JSON array), nullable `referenceId`, unique `auditEventId`, `createdAt`.
- `ClientReference`: `itemId`, `accountId`, `name`, `mime`, `size` (Int bytes), `sha256`, unique UUID `storageKey`, `createdAt`. Private PDF/image reference; never expose the storage key in portal DTOs.
- `ClientItemGrant`: `portalGrantId`, `itemId`, `view`, `comment`, `review`, `approve`, `download`, `version`, `changedById`, `reason`, `updatedAt`. Unique portalGrant/item. Capability removal is an audited version with all flags false, preserving history.
- `ClientItemGrantChange`: `grantId`, `grantVersion`, `actorId`, `reason`, `before`, `after`, unique `auditEventId`, `createdAt`; unique grant/version.
- `ClientReviewClassification`: `itemId`, `category` (`ClientRevisionCategory`: PREFERENCE_NEW_REQUIREMENT, EMPLOYEE_WORK_ERROR), `version`, `actorId`, `reason`, unique `auditEventId`, `createdAt`; unique item/version. Each change is a new immutable row.
- `ClientCorrection`: unique `itemId`, unique `correctionTaskId`, `actorId`, `reason`, unique `auditEventId`, `createdAt`.
- `ClientFinalApproval`: unique `packageId`, `accountId`, `signatureName`, `consent`, `credentialId`, nullable `ipAddress`, nullable `userAgent`, `itemSnapshot`, unique `auditEventId`, `createdAt`, nullable `closedAt`, nullable `retainUntil`. Retention is ten years after the earliest Completed/Cancelled project transition, independent of portal access.

Item `snapshot` is a JSON object with flat keys `taskId`, `taskVersion` (the resulting submitted task version), `title`, `description`, `ownerEmployeeId`, `targetDate` (YYYY-MM-DD), `startDate`, `estimatedHours`, `tags`, `checklist`, `planItemId`, `reviewRequirement`, `revisionId`, `drawingId`, `fileId`, `blobId`, `sha256`, `series`, `number`. Revision keys are null for a task-only item. Snapshot numeric/array/null values keep their JSON types. `label` is the client-facing item/revision label. Portal DTOs whitelist client-safe fields instead of returning persistence snapshots wholesale.

Final `itemSnapshot` is a JSON array sorted by item ID. Each entry has `itemId`, `itemVersion`, `decisionId`, `taskId`, `revisionId`, `drawingId`, `fileId`, `blobId`, `sha256`; nullable revision fields remain null. Canonical final evidence includes the identical array. The final passkey binding is `{ purpose: "CLIENT_FINAL_APPROVAL", packageId, packageVersion, items: itemSnapshot, signatureName, consent: true, comment }`. Item approval binding is `{ purpose: "CLIENT_ITEM_APPROVAL", itemId, itemVersion, snapshot, signatureName, consent: true, comment }`.

## Authorization and transaction boundary

Client commands lock and recheck in order: project, current account/session, portal grant, drawing/revision where relevant, task/package/item, credential. Managers also recheck current organization/employee/project assignment and cross-branch eligibility; project.view remains required. Stable ID order is required for multiple rows. Exact-item grant checks occur inside the same transaction as decisions. Every protected read/stream rechecks current account, session, grant expiry, item scope and project state.

Primary Clients view published items on their granted project and may invite/configure Additional Clients. Additional Clients receive only exact-item capability grants. Primary appointment remains Owner-only. Primary-only overall approval cannot be delegated; item approval may be delegated explicitly. Owner internal administration does not become a client decision identity.

Internal permissions: `client.review_manage`, `client.revision_classify`, `client.correction_assign`. Owner has ordinary Owner authority. Assigned Project Lead has review-management/correction-assignment authority subject to current project access. Other staff require their explicitly scoped permission. Classification requires Owner or explicit `client.revision_classify`; Project Lead assignment alone does not grant classification. Job titles confer no permission.

Business consumers catch passkey verification rejection inside the transaction callback, return a failure marker, commit consumed ceremony state, then reject outside the transaction. Do not expose a generic proof-to-approval endpoint. The consumer supplies the current immutable binding and must reject cancelled, On Hold, stale, withdrawn, expired, revoked or foreign-item commands. Formal approvals require Active project and current passkey UV proof; email codes alone are insufficient.

## Workflow and event interface

Events for producers: `client.review_sent`, `client.review_started`, `client.item_decided`, `client.commented`, `client.reference_uploaded`, `client.item_access_changed`, `client.revision_classified`, `client.correction_assigned`, `client.correction_resumed`, `client.final_approved`, `client.review_withdrawn`, and existing `client.approved` for each exact final revision fact. Root owns catalogue/recipient registration. All audits use subjectType matching the row (`client_review_package`, `client_review_item`, `client_item_decision`, `client_comment`, `client_reference`, `client_item_access`, `client_classification`, `client_correction`, `client_final_approval`) and include exact row IDs, projectId, relevant version and reason.

`client.review_sent` audits the package with `after.packageId`, `after.projectId`, `after.version`, `after.itemIds`. Each item has a `client.review_sent` audit with subject=itemId and `after.itemId`, `after.packageId`, `after.taskId`, `after.revisionId`, `after.version`, `after.snapshot`. Task mutations also write the existing TaskStatusChange and task.updated/task.completed/task.started canonical audit required by S09.

Item state: PENDING→IN_REVIEW; IN_REVIEW→APPROVED/REVISION_REQUIRED/REJECTED through one immutable decision after explicit review start. Cancellation withdraws only pending/in-review items and OPEN packages, preserving terminal item outcomes and all evidence. Corrections never replace old exact-item content: create a new item/package for the corrected submission and retain the earlier package and outcomes as history. Packages with negative item outcomes cannot become APPROVED.

Task state: BOTH INTERNAL_APPROVED→SUBMITTED_CLIENT_REVIEW; CLIENT IN_PROGRESS→SUBMITTED_CLIENT_REVIEW; SUBMITTED_CLIENT_REVIEW→IN_CLIENT_REVIEW; IN_CLIENT_REVIEW→CLIENT_APPROVED or CLIENT_REVISION_REQUIRED through the exact item decision (the service requires explicit review start and item IN_REVIEW). Only final overall package approval permits CLIENT_APPROVED→COMPLETED. CLIENT_REVISION_REQUIRED→IN_PROGRESS requires the linked correction task to be COMPLETED plus `client.correction_resumed` and ordinary task history/audit. S13 then permits a new internal review after that completed client-correction path; a drawing correction uses a distinct newer exact R/CR revision, never the old approved content.

Correction assignment/resumption addresses the latest published item for the task, ordered by immutable `snapshot.taskVersion`; earlier retained completed corrections cannot satisfy a later client revision request. Classification's command `expectedVersion` is the current item version; classification history has its own consecutive versions and does not change published item versions.

Item-level APPROVED decisions create no CLIENT RevisionApprovalFact. Only Primary final approval, after all package items are APPROVED and still bind current exact internally approved revisions, creates immutable final batch evidence and one canonical `client.approved` audit/fact per exact revision. S12 `s12_exact_evidence` requires projectId/drawingId/revisionId/blobId/sha256 and canonical delivery. Thus delegated item approval cannot bypass overall Primary approval or formal-issue gates.

The final consumer locks drawings for update and revisions for share before sorted tasks, package and items; the SQL guard retains the same exact tuples, current task status/content and post-wait grant authority. An authorized internal manager may complete a task only after the retained Primary batch. Canonical transition audits separately require the current exact item or management capability of their actor.

Formal handover uses existing S12 transmittals, exact items and named recipients. CLIENT recipients require a current grant and published Primary/exact Additional view scope for every issued PDF/image. Portal handover DTOs disclose only their scoped delivery and their own acknowledgement. Acknowledgements retain S12 own-recipient canonical evidence and require Active project/current session/current grant; cancellation allows earlier scoped issues and own approval history without new acknowledgements.

Classification records only the two approved categories and cannot directly change any performance score. Classification may target any CR revision item, or a negative R/task item. Every CR item requires a classification before final approval; an independent first CR does not require an invented correction origin. Correction tasks remain normal scoped CORRECTION tasks, linked to the source client item; assignment reason/audit is required.

Cancellation joins Owner-selected per-client future grant end times, pending item/package withdrawal and existing project/task cancellation in one transaction. After cancellation, client reads expose only previously formally issued PDF/image revisions within existing scope and that client's own approval history. No new decisions/comments/references/invitations; no drafts/CAD/internal performance/new transmittals. Expiry and revocation remain request-time checks. SQL guards use the current clock after waits, not transaction-start time.
