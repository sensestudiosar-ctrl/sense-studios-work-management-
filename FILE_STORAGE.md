## Storage and identity

- Each file has an opaque application ID and immutable storage key. Metadata records organization, one project/branch, folder, document number, revision series/number, checksum, MIME/size, current/approval/issue state, classification and access rules. Do not derive authorization from a URL or physical path.
- The NAS adapter uses a restricted service account and a configured root. It canonicalizes paths and rejects traversal/symlink escape; users never receive NAS credentials or unrestricted network shares. Internal workstations may still use NAS separately under firm policy, but app-controlled issues and approvals require registered files.
- Large PDF/image/DWG/SKP/RVT/Office/D5/Lumion/ZIP/video/audio files remain on NAS. Preview generation is optional and permission-bound; source files are not copied to cloud merely for convenience.
- Project creation can create versioned template folders through a retryable job. Folder creation failure is visible and recoverable rather than pretending success.

## Upload, revision and issue

1. Authenticate/authorize uploader and target project/folder. Validate name, declared/actual type, size policy and quota; stage streamed or chunked upload to a private temporary location.
2. Reassemble/resume chunks by upload ID, validate checksum and inspect dangerous content where practical. Finalize by atomic NAS rename or equivalent, then commit metadata/revision/audit/outbox. Reconcile orphaned staged/final files after failures; never overwrite an approved blob.
3. Revision reason is mandatory. R0/R1/... identify internal-review revisions; CR1/CR2/... identify client-review revisions under C-002 Q7. Current approved revision changes atomically in DB; previous is marked Superseded—Do Not Use, with historical storage intact during the approved retention period.
4. Client approval records exact item/revision and passkey-backed signature evidence. Formal issue creates immutable transmittal with recipients, time, purpose and acknowledgement status. Any correction is a new entry/version.
5. Download/view streams through the backend after a fresh permission check, except an Owner-issued native MinisCloud CAD share for an assigned vendor/consultant under C-006; use short-lived app-issued access only if it can be revoked effectively. Watermark protected previews and log access where needed. View-only controls can discourage copying, not guarantee screenshot prevention.
6. Public portfolio uses an explicit publication grant and separate public derivative. Private client gallery remains project scoped. No internal file becomes public automatically.

## External access and large files

The owner selected Cloudflare Tunnel for public app ingress (C-002 Q3); this does not finalize the deployment design or authorize direct NAS service exposure. Official documentation currently says publishing via Tunnel does not require a paid Access plan, but the Free proxied upload limit is 100 MB per request. Therefore app uploads must support resumable chunks below that limit. Cloudflare documents separate conditions for serving video and other large files through public Tunnel routes; large vendor CAD delivery must be tested on a separate controlled path. Clients receive PDF/image files only. Under C-013, once a project is Cancelled, the client portal serves only PDF/image revisions previously formally issued to that client and their own approval history, with a fresh permission and expiry check on every view/download. It never exposes drafts, internal records, new issues or new approval actions; C-014/C-015 end each client's project access on its own Owner-set date, with audited Owner changes or extensions; there is no shared period. Record retention continues independently of portal access. A C-024 lead-exit On Hold state blocks new issue and new client formal decisions while preserving existing issued files and authorized read access; Owner replacement and gate-checked resume are required before delivery continues. Under C-006, explicitly assigned vendors and consultants may receive a native MinisCloud link for one CAD file/revision, issued only by the Owner, expiring after seven days and protected by an extraction code. The app records recipient, file/revision, issuer, issue/expiry and cancellation; on access loss the Owner cancels every active link in MinisCloud immediately and records proof. Under C-012, Owner project cancellation ends vendor/consultant access: inventory active links, confirm each native link is revoked and record that confirmation before finalizing cancellation; the app then denies vendor/consultant project access. If native revocation cannot be confirmed, keep the cancellation request pending and surface the blocker. Later closeout access requires a separate C-016 approval by the assigned Project Lead or explicitly authorized Team Leader, limited to the named work package/files and an end time. The Owner still alone issues any native CAD link under C-006. Native links can be forwarded, so recipients must be told not to share them. Do not expose SMB, database, NAS administration or unrestricted folders. Do not assume the app can create or revoke MinisCloud links programmatically; verify actual MinisCloud controls on the installed OS before portal launch.

## Backup and reconciliation

Back up PostgreSQL metadata, NAS files and revision evidence together every night to the Owner's always-reachable, physically separate computer/NAS, encrypted in transit and at rest (C-007). The accepted worst-case data loss after total primary-NAS loss is one day. Record a consistent backup point and rehearse restoring an approved revision with its metadata; monitor failures. NAS snapshots/versioning help local rollback but do not replace the offsite copy. C-003/C-009 retain approval evidence and issued drawings for ten years from project close, audit for ten years from event time, exited-employee records for seven years after exit and site selfies for ninety days from capture. Scheduled expiry gives advance Owner notice, honors documented holds, and accounts for backup lifecycle and deletion proof. Legal/contract fit remains a production gate. Alert on checksum mismatch, missing file, orphaned blob, failed transmittal, or backup/restore failure.
