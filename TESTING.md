## Critical E2E journeys

1. Owner creates branch, team, grants and employee; employee sees only assigned cross-branch work; revoked access fails after refresh and direct API retry.
2. Lead jumps from New to Proposal with a reason; Contacted and Meeting are recorded as skipped, not completed, and ungranted/cross-branch attempts are denied. A returning Lost enquiry creates a linked new lead while the original Lost reason and historical report remain unchanged. A real scope change moves a nonterminal lead from Negotiation back to Proposal with a reason and prior activity intact; Won lead converts to Design project with one Project Lead and template stages/tasks; scope change preserves baseline. Project Lead cancellation request cannot change state; Owner cancellation from Draft/Active/On Hold requires reason, atomically stops unfinished delivery tasks, keeps completed tasks and all time/review history, removes stopped work from overdue/active capacity, and permits a distinct linked closeout task only with assigned Project Lead or explicitly authorized Team Leader approval; client portal switches to issued-record read-only mode with required per-client end dates and pending decisions are withdrawn; vendor/consultant project access ends, and any active native CAD link is verified revoked before cancellation finalizes.
3. Employee self-task versus manager assignment, dependency warning, waiting reason, extension request, approved deadline reason/audit.
4. Design project: task submission → internal review → correction → new revision/round → client CR-series item review/partial approval with passkey-backed signature after internal R-series review → final issue/transmittal/handover; old revision remains traceable.
5. NAS upload/download permission: large chunked upload, forbidden direct path/ID, confidential view-only, superseded revision warning.
6. Time timer/manual entry/correction with reason, then accounting export retry without duplicate.
7. Branch-specific January–December leave credit, new-hire/transfer calendar-day proration, five-day carry-forward/expiry and leave approval reduces capacity and flags critical tasks; cancellation request alone changes nothing, while same-level approval restores only eligible unused leave/capacity with audit and affected-task alerts; site attendance captures only on-duty evidence.
8. Turnkey project through BOQ/material/procurement coordination, factory/site progress, manpower, daily report, snag verifier assignment, failed-check reopen with retained evidence, denied fixer self-verification, denied verifier-only closure, authorized management closure with passed evidence, handover.
9. Portal isolation for primary/additional client, consultant and vendor/factory; item-only delegated client approval, Primary Client final authority, client PDF/image restriction, cancelled-project issued-record and own-approval-history read-only access with denied draft/new-review/other-client/expired attempts, two clients with different end dates, and audited Owner-approved extension, and Owner-controlled vendor/consultant native CAD share expiry/revocation, including cancelled-project denial, C-016 narrow expiring closeout access, denied ungranted Team Leader/ordinary employee approval, and Owner-only CAD issuance.
10. Site PWA offline draft/photo/snag sync after reconnection, conflict surfaced, duplicate replay ignored.
11. Employee exit revokes access immediately, moves each unfinished task to Waiting while preserving the departing owner and prior state, denies further task progress until an authorized replacement is chosen, and keeps completed-task history unchanged; an Active project led by the leaver moves On Hold and blocks new delivery decisions until the Owner appoints an eligible replacement and resumes it (C-024).
11. Performance and project-health score changes show underlying evidence; external delay does not penalize employee; management-only rankings stay hidden from employees/portals.
12. Human rejects high-impact automation/AI suggestion; no underlying assignment/deadline/client communication changes.
