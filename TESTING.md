4. Design project: task submission → internal review → correction → new immutable revision/round → client CR-series item review/partial approval with passkey-backed signature after internal R-series review → final issue/transmittal/handover; old revision remains traceable.
5. NAS upload/download permission: large chunked upload, forbidden direct path/ID, confidential view-only, superseded revision warning.
6. Time timer/manual entry/correction with reason, then accounting export retry without duplicate.
7. 8.
